What these are
--------------
Short reference recordings the local VoxCPM2 server clones from. They ship so a new install can
dub immediately instead of opening an empty Voice Profiles panel. The app reads them in place
from this directory and never copies them into the customer's data folder.

Where they came from
--------------------
Collected by the developer from their own media library and imported into the development
build's voice bank one at a time. They are recordings of real human voices. No per-clip release
or consent record accompanies them, and shipping them redistributes those voices to every
customer. That is a known and accepted exposure, recorded here so it is not discovered later.

Replacing a voice is a source change and a rebuild: swap the clip in the development bank and
re-run `npm run assets:voice-profiles`. Nothing in the app pins a clip by content.
