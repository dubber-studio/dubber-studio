LICENSING — READ BEFORE SHIPPING
This set was bulk-collected from khmerfonts.info, an index site rather than a licensing channel,
and it carries no per-font licence record. A scan of the OpenType `name` tables found roughly 157
files declaring the SIL Open Font Licence, ~759 asserting "All rights reserved", and ~400
mentioning "commercial" — frequently in a "free for personal use, contact the author for
commercial use" clause. The remainder declare nothing machine-readable.

Redistribution rights for the bulk of these faces are therefore unverified. Narrowing to a
cleared subset is a data change and nothing more: drop the unwanted entries from the collection,
re-run its build-index.js and check-coverage.js, and re-run this script. No app code, schema or
interface depends on which faces are present.
